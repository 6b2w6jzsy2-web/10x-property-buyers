About 10X Property Buyers

A straightforward approach to buying property

We help owners explore a direct sale without pressure, unclear wording or unnecessary delay.

Our approach

Clear enough for sellers. Professional enough for the legal process.

Property sales can become difficult when there is a chain, repair work, probate, tenants or a tight timescale. We provide another option by assessing the property directly and explaining the likely process.

We do not claim a direct sale is right for everyone. Some owners may be better suited to the open market. We believe the best approach is to explain the trade-off clearly so you can make an informed choice.
