About us

A straightforward approach to buying property

10X Property Buyers helps owners explore a direct sale without pressure, unclear wording or unnecessary delay.

What we aim to do

Property sales can become difficult when there is a chain, repair work, probate, tenants or a tight timescale. We provide another option by assessing the property directly and explaining the likely process.

We do not claim a direct offer is right for everyone. Some owners may be better suited to the open market. We believe the best approach is to explain the trade-off clearly so you can make an informed choice.

Our approach

  • Clear communication
  • No pressure to accept
  • Respect for your preferred timescale
  • Simple explanations
  • Independent solicitors for both sides
  • Offers subject to proper checks

What matters to us

Clarity

We explain what the offer means and what happens next.

Respect

You can take time, ask questions and compare your options.

Delivery

Once agreed, we work with solicitors to keep the transaction moving.
