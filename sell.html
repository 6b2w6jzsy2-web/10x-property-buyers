Is it right for you?

When a direct sale can help

A direct purchase can suit owners who want a clear buyer without repeated viewings or an estate agent chain.

  • You need a quicker or more controlled timescale
  • The property needs repair or updating
  • You are dealing with probate or an inherited home
  • You are a landlord selling with or without tenants
  • A previous sale has fallen through
  • You want a more private process
An estate agent may achieve a higher open-market price. A direct sale is normally chosen for simplicity, privacy and control.
