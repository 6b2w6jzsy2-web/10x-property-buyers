How it works

Clear steps from enquiry to completion

We explain each stage before you commit, and you are under no obligation after making an enquiry.

01

Send the details

Provide the address, property type, condition and your ideal timescale.

02

We review the property

We look at local evidence, likely refurbishment, legal matters and the purchase position.

03

We discuss the offer

You receive a clear offer and can ask questions or compare other routes.

04

Solicitors are appointed

Both parties instruct independent solicitors and the legal checks begin.

05

Survey and final checks

Access may be required so the purchase can be checked before exchange.

06

Exchange and completion

Once contracts are agreed, the sale completes on the agreed date.

What you will need

  • Proof of identity and address
  • Details of all legal owners
  • Property title information where available
  • Mortgage or secured lending details
  • Tenancy details if the property is let
  • Probate documents where relevant

Important to know

Any initial offer is subject to inspection, legal checks, title, funding and contract. You should take independent legal advice before entering a binding agreement.
