Is a direct sale right for you?

A direct sale can suit owners who want a clear buyer without open-market viewings or an estate agent chain.

  • You need a quicker or more controlled timescale
  • The property needs repairs or full refurbishment
  • You are dealing with probate or an inherited property
  • You are a landlord selling with or without tenants
  • A previous sale has fallen through
  • You value privacy and fewer viewings

It is important to compare your options. An open-market sale may achieve a higher price, while a direct offer may provide more certainty and less work.
