Topographical Surveys built around your project

CSL Surveying provides topographical surveys for clients across the United Kingdom. We review the proposed survey area, access, project stage and intended use before confirming a suitable method and deliverable.

What we can provide

Planned fieldwork

Survey activities planned around access, site risks, programme and the quality of information required.

Modern capture

Suitable survey instruments and methods selected to match the site and project brief.

Useful outputs

Clear CAD, PDF, point cloud or model outputs prepared for practical use by your team.

Quality and limitations

Survey results are checked before issue. Any key assumptions, access restrictions, limitations or areas requiring further investigation are clearly identified in the deliverables or supporting notes.

Who we support

Our clients include contractors, consultants, developers, design teams, local authorities, asset owners and property teams working on projects of all sizes.
